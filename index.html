READING THE EVIDENCE

Reported mass needs context.

Scope & preparation

Local National Pollutant Inventory snapshot, 2007/08–2024/25. Only substances eligible for air reporting and finite air values are included. Kilograms are converted to tonnes. Missing values are excluded, never filled with zero.

Limits & interpretation

Substance categories can overlap. Their sum is not unique pollutant mass, toxicity, exposure or health risk. Missing reports do not mean zero emissions. Industry trends can reflect changes in reporting and facility coverage.

Railton Works' unusually large value is retained as supplied. Inspect its substance breakdown and original report; this snapshot has not been independently certified against a corrected inventory.
